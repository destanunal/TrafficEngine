**Traffic Engine** brings customizable roads, working traffic lights, signs and modern street equipment to Minecraft.

From neighborhood streets to busy intersections and highway networks—build your city, your way.

Inspired by [TrafficCraft](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trafficcraft), with expanded customization and new infrastructure.

***

## 🛣️ Roads & Markings

*   **Road Building:** Six asphalt types, matching slopes, sidewalks, curbs and speed bumps.
*   **Road Painting:** Create lane lines, arrows, crossings, smooth curves, roundabouts and connected markings across multiple blocks.
*   **Area Painting:** Paint rectangular, triangular or custom quadrilateral areas with solid colors or hatching.
*   **Custom Patterns:** Draw your own markings, import PNG images and save favorites.

## 🚦 Traffic Lights & Control

*   **Modern Traffic Lights:** Circular lenses, realistic housings, vertical and horizontal layouts, adjustable lamps, symbols and countdowns.
*   **Double-Sided Lights:** Independent settings and controller connections for each face.
*   **Custom Lamp Symbols:** Use arrows, pedestrian and bicycle symbols, or individual custom drawings.
*   **Traffic Controllers:** Build synchronized intersections with configurable timing and coordinated crossing signals.
*   **Warning Lights:** Custom colors, single or dual lamps, adjustable layouts, flashing sequences and Redstone control.

## 🪧 Signs & Displays

*   **Traffic Signs:** Flexible mounting, angled placement and double-sided designs.
*   **Road & Highway Signs:** Multiple sizes with editable text, arrows, colors and layouts.
*   **Curve Direction Signs:** Single or multiple arrows, selectable colors, diagonal placement and connecting wide panels.
*   **Custom Sign Workbench:** Design and save your own traffic signs.
*   **Electronic Displays:** Multicolor LED screens, scrolling information displays and configurable speed limits.

## 🚧 Roadside Equipment

*   **Poles & Lighting:** Standard and curved traffic poles, flexible equipment mounting and customizable colored street lamps.
*   **Railings, Gates & Safety Barriers:** Connecting pedestrian railings, sloped sections, matching gates and reflective wire mesh barriers.
*   **Bollards & Access Control:** Retractable hydraulic bollards, parking barriers and foldable barriers with supported wrench or Redstone controls.
*   **Street Details:** Guardrails, bicycle lane separators, illuminated traffic island bollards and pedestrian request buttons.

## 🎨 Tools & Optimization

*   **Modern Interfaces:** Clean menus, intuitive controls and live previews.
*   **Drawing Tools:** Pixel editors with adjustable pencil and eraser sizes, plus PNG import in supported editors.
*   **Survival Ready:** Craft road materials and traffic equipment in Survival mode.
*   **Performance-Focused:** Cached models, bounded caches and event-driven updates help reduce repeated work.
