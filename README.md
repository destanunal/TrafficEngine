A comprehensive traffic, road, and city-management mod designed to bring modern Minecraft cities to life.

Traffic Engine is inspired by **[TrafficCraft](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trafficcraft)** and expands its foundation with new building tools, dynamic mechanics, enhanced customization, realistic road textures, and optimized performance.

***

## **✨ Key Features**

*   **Realistic Asphalt Variants:** Includes 6 asphalt styles—Default, Damaged, Heavy Damaged, Light, Dark, and Dirty—with seamless random texture tiling. All five additional asphalt types also include slope versions.
*   **Realistic Speed Bumps:** Functional Flat and Wide Speed Bumps with directional placement, accurate hitboxes, improved models, and proper inventory appearances.
*   **Flexible Sign Placement:** Traffic Signs support double-sided and wall-mounted modes. Signs attached to Traffic Poles can also use angled placement.
*   **Horizontal and Diagonal Traffic Systems:** Includes Horizontal Traffic Lights and automatically adjusting Diagonal Traffic Lights and Traffic Signs based on placement direction.
*   **Traffic LED Panel:** A programmable LED panel with customizable pixel patterns and colors.
*   **Traffic Information Display:** A large programmable display for road information, warnings, and custom messages.
*   **Traffic Lights with Digital Countdown:** Realistic traffic lights featuring dynamic countdown displays, synchronized schedules, and improved world-transition handling.
*   **Bike Lane Improvements:** Includes a new Bike Lane Divider and support for applying bicycle logos, pedestrian logos, lines, and other markings without removing the existing painted surface.
*   **Pattern Catalogue System:** Change the shape and decal of Traffic Signs instantly using Pattern Catalogue items.
*   **Smart Traffic Lights and Street Lamps:** Configure and synchronize intersections using the Traffic Light Linker, Wrench, and Street Lamp Config Card.
*   **New Turnstile Barrier:** Adds a functional new barrier model for stations, controlled areas, and modern city builds.
*   **Expanded Traffic Signs:** A large collection of international road signs.

***

## **🔄 Seamless Migration from TrafficCraft!**

Already have a large city built with the original **TrafficCraft**? No problem!

Traffic Engine includes a built-in **automatic migration system**. Remove the old mod, install Traffic Engine, and load your world. Your existing roads, signs, custom patterns, and linked traffic lights will automatically convert to **Traffic Engine** without losing their data.
