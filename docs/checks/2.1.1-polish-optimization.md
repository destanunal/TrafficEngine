# Traffic Engine 2.1.1 — corrected supports and safe optimizations

## Current behavior

- Small road signs have the same two-rail, two-spine rear frame as the other road boards. Their freestanding, connected and hanging variants use one centered connection between the board and the carrier. Side-mounted boards retain one central post connection. The compact carrier ends at the central connection.
- All road-board hardware uses the existing traffic-sign-post texture. Custom face corners now follow Minecraft FaceInfo order, so U/V coordinates retain the pole's orientation and physical scale. Long pieces tile in at most 16-pixel lengths rather than stretching/resetting at arbitrary divisions. No new bitmap is introduced.
- Curved mast faces keep the four semantic sides of a straight post through the bend. Their shade reference no longer switches abruptly to UP/DOWN halfway around the arc. Geometry, collision and placement footprint are unchanged.
- Inventory models use the requested ordinary right-facing angle [30,225,0]. Small and medium board scales are 0.40 and 0.38; the large board uses 0.20. Translations center the rotated bounds inside the slot. Handheld, ground and fixed displays retain their previous transforms.
- Railing erase clicks play the ordinary main-hand swing animation.
- Diagonal and tilted traffic signs draw twelve cached plate edges directly into vanilla's existing selection buffer. Posts are outlined separately where present; two-sided boards retain both plate outlines. Special dynamic-image boards also receive cached simple outlines. Picking/collision voxel volumes are unchanged. Tilted bounds use the actual authored rigid transform and pivot, removing the previous visual overshoot from the sliced approximation.

## Performance, memory and resource changes

- TrafficSignShape previously rebuilt a four-direction array and combined voxel shapes on every ordinary lookup. Its finite shape/facing combinations are now built once and shared by all block states. Shape volumes are checked against the previous constructor for exact equality.
- The outline cache is bounded by the finite enum/facing/mount combinations and populated only when targeted. Warm drawing does no geometry construction, trigonometry, world reads or extra rendering pass.
- Single-contributor RGB sections publish their already immutable source map directly, eliminating the duplicate retained hash table. Multi-source sections still merge into fresh immutable snapshots. Replacing/removing sources never mutates old published maps. Contributor identity sets start with a smaller capacity and can grow normally.
- RGB traversal checks its visited cache before querying chunk availability, reducing repeated chunk lookups while preserving loading and occlusion rules.
- Three inventory models inherit their board geometry. Eight additional material variants inherit strictly identical geometry, with their existing texture bindings retained. Before writing these eight variants, every resolved model field was checked for equality against its original definition.
- The eleven deduplicated resource files remove 92,791 bytes of repeated JSON (about 90.6 KiB). An isolated DEFLATE estimate for these resources is 8,583 bytes saved; this is not a measurement of final JAR size, which also includes changed classes. No packaging task or JAR was produced.
- No configuration, quality, range, light strength, gameplay setting or content ID is changed by these optimizations.

## Validation

- Minecraft 1.20.1: common, Fabric and Forge compileJava passed.
- Minecraft 1.21.1: common, Fabric and NeoForge compileJava passed.
- Native model/outline checks compare the actual authored plate bounds in both orientations and every facing, verify twelve-edge cached outlines, and verify unchanged picking/collision volumes.
- All 1,344 hardware faces match Minecraft's native FaceInfo UV corner association and physical pole-texture scale. The 24 road-board variants retain straight/diagonal parity; all three inventory projections fit inside a 14.4-pixel region of their 16-pixel slots.
- Existing 5,604-assertion update checks passed, including 1,024 road-sign states and 102,400 shape lookups without world reads.
- RGB tests passed for four-facing LED half-space restriction, opaque walls, source merging/removal, immutable old snapshots and preserved external brightness.
- In the maximum-radius single-source fixture, immutable section sharing avoids 30,752 bytes of duplicate short-array payload (object headers excluded). This is a fixture result, not a total Minecraft RAM measurement.
- A 100,000-lookup warm shape loop measured 200 bytes of thread allocation on each Minecraft version. This does not measure FPS or whole-game allocation.

The checks do not start Minecraft. In-game appearance, live mixin integration, FPS, whole-game RAM and final packaged JAR size have not been measured.
