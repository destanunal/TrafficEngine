# Traffic Engine 2.0.1 — local changes and validation

Target: Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric and Forge.

## Changes

- Traffic light supports, collars, previews and items now use the existing sign-pole texture at the same physical UV scale as the pole.
- Horizontal traffic lights retain cardinal-only placement. The cancelled four diagonal bodies are removed; legacy diagonal states also render with cardinal housing, lenses and shared collision geometry.
- The pedestrian request button uses the standard pole UV scale. All rear shell/end-cap planes clear the pole; two visible metal straps wrap around the pole and connect to its rear plate.
- Hydraulic bollards use Minecraft's existing light-gray concrete material for a simpler, quiet gray finish. Three real cap quads replace eight degenerate centre-fan faces, removing the tiny central seam and reducing the cap face count. No bitmap was added.
- Matching gates stacked vertically connect their bars and share manual opening, closing, wrench color changes and Redstone activation. Differently oriented columns remain independent. Operation inspects only the loaded column on edits, with a maximum of 1,024 segments; normal rendering and shape queries do not inspect terrain.
- Turkish name: **Çift Taraflı Trafik Lambası**. English and German already use the corresponding double-sided name.
- Railing rails meet the central post below its cap; overlapping internal end/top faces are removed, including lowered and stacked variants.
- Countdown digits fit their complete bounding rectangle inside the circular lens. A finite nine-layout scale table is computed once and reused by vertical, horizontal and independent double-sided lamps.
- The curved mast now matches the standard pole's constant 2x2 square section, uses a quarter-circle bend and ends in a genuinely horizontal arm. Its underside meets the full width of the existing lamp carrier. Shared vertices form a closed mesh within the original six-cell footprint; static authored faces decrease from **134 in 2.0 to 58**.
- Curved-pole side UVs now follow the actual multipart sign post's length-by-width sampling. Front/back UVs are oriented along the mast rather than across its width; the inventory model shares this mapping. Geometry and face count are unchanged.
- Sloped railing models and their inventory item now sample the same metal atlas at the physical scale used by straight railings; no extra geometry or texture is added.

## Validation

- Affected common and platform **compileJava** tasks pass for Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1. Resource processing is checked. No packaging or release JAR tasks were requested or run.
- Asset checks across both versions cover multipart selection, native coordinate and UV bounds, ordered bounds, matching texture scale, gate seams, mount clearance and the closed mast mesh.
- `docs/checks/Update201Check.java`: native model parsing, 48 shared horizontal/mount outlines, 16 shared gate outlines, loaded-only bounded column handling, manual/color propagation and Redstone state changes. A three-segment gate writes exactly three states and emits one sound/event; unchanged power emits none.
- Every countdown from 1 through 999 fits within a radius of 1.826 model units, leaving margin inside the radius-2 lens.
- Existing curved-pole and railing/slope regression checks are retained and the gate outline expectation is updated for stacking.
- These are compilation, model and targeted logic checks. In-game visual review, real multiplayer integration and FPS profiling remain to be performed; no zero-FPS-impact claim is made.

Compile-only invocation: `gradlew :common:compileJava :fabric:compileJava :forge:compileJava :common:processResources -x :common:jar --no-daemon --max-workers=2 --console=plain -I docs/checks/compile191.init.gradle`.
