# **🆕 Traffic Engine Update 2.1**

This update expands road sign options, improves mounting connections and texture consistency and enhances roadside equipment with various visual fixes.

---

## **🚦 Traffic Lights**

- Mounting brackets now use the same texture as traffic sign poles.
- Countdown numbers—including two- and three-digit values—now fit inside circular lenses.

---

## **🪧 Road Signs**

- The names of existing directional signs have been changed to “Small Road Sign” and “Road Sign.” A new 4×3-block “Large Road Sign” has been added, featuring four configurable lines, a default of four lines, and larger default text.
- Newly placed “Small Road Signs” are now two-line by default.
- The text size on “Small Road Sign” and “Road Sign” panels can now be increased up to 200%. For the “Large Road Sign,” this limit is 400%.
- Sign panels are now slightly thinner and feature colored metal back surfaces with shallow metal support profiles.
- The rear supports have been adjusted to prevent them from overlapping with the mounting poles.
- Missing or jagged edges on road sign panels have been corrected.
- Softer off-white colors are now used on white panels to reduce excessive glare.

---

## **🚧 Roadside Equipment**

- Curved Traffic Poles are now compatible with the thickness, texture, and texture scale of standard traffic sign poles.
- The geometry and mounting connections of curved poles have been improved to create a smoother pole shaft and eliminate gaps.
- The ends of curved poles are now aligned with standard traffic sign poles for horizontal extensions.
- Suspended mounting brackets are now visible only when compatible equipment is installed.
- Curved poles now support traffic sign poles, traffic signs, electronic speed limit signs, and LED displays.
- Hydraulic poles now feature a simpler and cleaner texture.
- Pedestrian call buttons now feature visible metal mounting clamps.
- Overlapping surfaces between request button housings and poles have been corrected.

---

## **🚶 Pedestrian Railings and Gates**

- Vertically stacked Pedestrian Railing Gates now connect to each other and open and close together; this includes Redstone control.
- Railing gates and sloped railings now use textures compatible with standard traffic sign poles.
- Overlapping surfaces that could cause jittering on railing tops and connection points have been fixed.

---

## **🛠️ Improvements and Fixes**

- Curved post geometry has been simplified while maintaining its proper shape.
- Existing textures were reused without adding new texture files.
- Gate updates were limited to connected and loaded segments.
- Cached sign geometry was reused, and temporary arrays were removed from the sign creation process.
