# Traffic Engine 2.0: menu and railing polish validation

Applied to both Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.

## Changes

- Menu captions use visible source-pixel bounds for bitmap and Unihex glyphs. The final text origin is snapped once to framebuffer pixels, after centering. Measurements are bounded to 1,024 captions and 4,096 glyphs and invalidated on font/resource or language changes. Shared buttons, tabs, multiline favorite captions, fields and menu headings use these measurements.
- Missing localized painting control captions were filled. The Area Painting entry button has eight extra logical units of width. Its dialog uses the slightly smaller 536 × 330 layout.
- Traffic light lenses and symbols again use the original unshaded text material with full-bright lightmap values when lit. Off lenses keep the world lightmap. No additional rendering pass was introduced by this material correction.
- Localized request-panel labels use native, uniform two-pixel strokes without uneven horizontal image resampling.
- The railing inventory model uses two end posts and the world model's rail/picket dimensions, retaining its right-facing inventory transform.
- Railing up/down connections update on placement and neighboring block changes. Joined upper pickets meet the row below and intermediate post caps/bottom rails are omitted. No block entity, ticker or world scan was added.
- Railing destruction particles select the gray or charcoal atlas sprite from the broken state, including after platform-specific terrain particle sprite selection.

- Traffic pole bases include the inventory model's pole when placed on their own, with matching selection/collision and nine shared cached shapes across terrain depths. The concrete foot is reduced from 8 × 6 × 8 to 7 × 5 × 7 model units; the pole still reaches the same block height. Existing connection properties and slope embedding remain compatible.

## Latest signal-menu and housing corrections (2026-10-25)

- The preset icon picker derives its neutral-color silhouettes from the actual lamp PNGs, using the same off-lens comparison as world lamp masks. None remains blank. At most eight immutable masks are cached and invalidated with font/resource reloads; rendering reuses merged rectangles and the existing GUI buffer.
- One to four signal rows now fit without scrolling at normal supported GUI layouts. Per-bulb symbol/custom drawing editors are opened by 20-unit pencil buttons beside the matching live-preview lamps. Keyboard access and localized tooltips are retained.
- Each signal choice renders its own saved symbol or custom mask. The live preview renders the actual baked world body with its saved body paint, full-bright lightmap and built-in inventory lighting.
- Existing traffic-light body models use matte gray concrete and slim matte black visors. Rear spacers and visor thickness/projection were reduced without increasing cuboid counts. Front lamp coordinates and tint/pole references are retained.
- Latest `:common:compileJava` passed on both versions after these changes. The previously recorded full loader compilation matrix predates this last common-only rendering/layout adjustment.
- `PoleBaseShapeCheck`: 185 assertions passed per version, covering all 36 depth/connection/water states, JSON geometry against native shape bounds, inventory/world parity and the nine-shape cache.
- `SignalMenuCheck`: 3,705 assertions passed per version, covering the seven actual lamp icon silhouettes pixel-for-pixel, merged rectangle coverage, cache invalidation, blank None and compact row/pencil bounds at normal GUI sizes.

These checks do not confirm the final live preview or housing appearance in an actual game window. External dynamic-light mod combinations, the unprovided 130-mod pack, and measured FPS/frame-time comparisons remain unverified.
