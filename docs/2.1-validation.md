# Traffic Engine 2.1 — local changes and validation

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric and NeoForge.

## Changes

- Traffic light supports, collars, previews and items now use the existing sign-pole texture at the same physical UV scale as the pole.
- Horizontal traffic lights retain cardinal-only placement. The cancelled four diagonal bodies are removed; legacy diagonal states also render with cardinal housing, lenses and shared collision geometry.
- The pedestrian request button uses the standard pole UV scale. All rear shell/end-cap planes clear the pole; two visible metal straps wrap around the pole and connect to its rear plate.
- Hydraulic bollards use Minecraft's existing light-gray concrete material for a simpler, quiet gray finish. Three real cap quads replace eight degenerate centre-fan faces, removing the tiny central seam and reducing the cap face count. No bitmap was added.
- Matching gates stacked vertically connect their bars and share manual opening, closing, wrench color changes and Redstone activation. Differently oriented columns remain independent. Operation inspects only the loaded column on edits, with a maximum of 1,024 segments; normal rendering and shape queries do not inspect terrain.
- Turkish name: **Çift Taraflı Trafik Lambası**. English and German already use the corresponding double-sided name.
- Railing rails meet the central post below its cap; overlapping internal end/top faces are removed, including lowered and stacked variants.
- Countdown digits fit their complete bounding rectangle inside the circular lens. A finite nine-layout scale table is computed once and reused by vertical, horizontal and independent double-sided lamps.
- The curved mast now matches the standard pole's constant 2x2 square section, uses a quarter-circle bend and ends in a genuinely horizontal arm. Its underside meets the full width of the existing lamp carrier. Shared vertices form a closed mesh within the original six-cell footprint; static authored faces decrease from **134 in 2.0 to 58**.
- Curved-pole side UVs now follow the actual multipart sign post's length-by-width sampling. Front/back UVs are oriented along the mast rather than across its width; the inventory model shares this mapping. Geometry and face count are unchanged.
- Sloped railing models and their inventory item now sample the same metal atlas at the physical scale used by straight railings; no extra geometry or texture is added.

## Validation

- Affected common and platform **compileJava** tasks pass for Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1. Resource processing is checked. No packaging or release JAR tasks were requested or run.
- Asset checks across both versions cover multipart selection, native coordinate and UV bounds, ordered bounds, matching texture scale, gate seams, mount clearance and the closed mast mesh.
- `docs/checks/Update201Check.java`: native model parsing, 48 shared horizontal/mount outlines, 16 shared gate outlines, loaded-only bounded column handling, manual/color propagation and Redstone state changes. A three-segment gate writes exactly three states and emits one sound/event; unchanged power emits none.
- Every countdown from 1 through 999 fits within a radius of 1.826 model units, leaving margin inside the radius-2 lens.
- Existing curved-pole and railing/slope regression checks are retained and the gate outline expectation is updated for stacking.
- These are compilation, model and targeted logic checks. In-game visual review, real multiplayer integration and FPS profiling remain to be performed; no zero-FPS-impact claim is made.

Compile-only invocation: `gradlew :common:compileJava :fabric:compileJava :neoforge:compileJava :common:processResources -x :common:jar --no-daemon --max-workers=2 --console=plain -I docs/checks/compile191.init.gradle`.

## Horizontal post junction correction

- The upper arm now follows the standard post centerline (local Y=8), reaches the terminal cell boundary, and exposes a horizontal post connection. A short downward socket preserves existing lamp mounting. The six saved cell indices and 24 cached shapes are unchanged.
- The revised closed mesh uses 58 static faces, no block entity, extra renderer, world scan or recurring update. Item bounds retain their previous size.
- New small direction signs start with two rows. Existing saved row choices and legacy layouts retain their loading behavior.

- Sign artwork clearance increased from 0.001 to 1/256 block; the existing four cached depth layers retain their relative ordering with two additional bias units. This prevents the baked casing competing with the thin front frame at distance/oblique angles, without widening the border, adding vertices, render passes or per-frame calculations. In-game visual confirmation remains pending.

## Conditional lamp socket

- The curved arm now has a bare endpoint by default, including its item model. A lamp directly below the terminal cell enables the downward socket; removing the lamp hides it again. Vertical, horizontal and double-sided traffic lights are supported.
- A block-state model variant selects the closed bare or mounted mesh in the existing chunk render path. Only downward neighbour changes inspect the supplied neighbour state; no new ticker, scan, rendering pass or network packet is added. Collision shapes are precomputed in a bounded table of 28 shared shapes, including four mounted endpoints.

## Highway sign rear finish

- Highway panels retain their selected theme color on a metal-textured rear face, with two shallow horizontal mounting rails and two vertical stiffeners. Ground, connected-post, side-mounted and item models share this finish.
- Four static elements add 16 visible faces per model; hidden front faces and vertical end caps are omitted. Existing metal textures are reused, with no additional texture, render pass, block-state property, ticker or per-frame code. Front artwork and mounts are preserved. In-game visual confirmation remains pending.

## Sign and mounting refinements

- Direction panel thickness reduced from 0.625 to 0.5 model units, preserving the artwork plane. Rear profiles are 0.25 units deep, ending before the pole face on side-mounted models. Small panels share the metal rear finish.
- Curved endpoint equipment detection shares a constant-time state predicate across placement, survival, neighbour updates and existing LED legacy repair. Lower sockets update only on neighbour changes; no new ticker, scan, texture or render pass.
- White background/panel colors reduced from DEE2DC/E7EAE4 to CDD1CB/D6D9D3. Electronic speed display colors are unchanged.
- In-game visual confirmation remains pending.

## Large Road Sign

- Added `large_road_direction_sign`, retaining both existing registry IDs. Physical panel 64×48×0.5 model units (4×3 blocks); side mounts retain the same artwork depth and pole clearance. Four shallow rear profiles reuse the sign metal texture, tinted rear surface preserves the panel theme.
- New placements default to four rows and 200% lettering; Large Road Signs allow 70–400%, while other sign sizes allow 70–200%. Existing panels remain limited to their original two/three rows and old saved layouts retain their rows. Fourth destination, distance, arrow and panel color serialize through NBT and the existing update packet.
- One existing block entity renderer; fixed four-row storage and bounded precomputed frames for three physical sizes. Collision shapes are cached; connection invalidation only checks loaded touching neighbors at the panel's width. Removed temporary destination/arrow arrays from every rendered sign frame. No new textures, tickers or network channels.
- Item transforms retain the existing rightward viewing angle at a compact inventory/held size. Four-row editor uses the shared glyph-bounds centering cache and a larger content area with no scrolling.
- Large custom face corners allow a bounded -32..64 model-unit range; native JSON element proxy bounds stay within Minecraft's -16..32 range.
- Compilation and native geometry/data checks recorded below. Visual in-game confirmation remains pending.
