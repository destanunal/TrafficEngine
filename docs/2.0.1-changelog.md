# **🆕 Traffic Engine Update 2.0.1**

This update refines roadside equipment, improves mounting connections and texture consistency, and fixes several visual issues.

---

## **🚦 Traffic Lights**

- Mounting supports now use the same texture as traffic sign poles.
- Countdown numbers now fit inside circular lenses, including two- and three-digit values.

---

## **🚧 Roadside Equipment**

- Curved Traffic Poles now match the thickness, texture, and texture scale of standard traffic sign poles.
- Curved pole geometry and lamp connections have been refined to eliminate gaps and create a smoother mast arm.
- Hydraulic Bollards now use a simpler, cleaner texture.
- Fixed a small visual artifact at the center of bollard caps.
- Pedestrian request buttons now feature visible metal mounting clamps.
- Fixed overlapping surfaces between request button housings and their poles.

---

## **🚶 Pedestrian Railings & Gates**

- Vertically stacked Pedestrian Railing Gates now connect and open or close together, including through Redstone control.
- Railing gates and sloped railings now use textures consistent with standard traffic sign poles.
- Fixed overlapping surfaces that could cause flickering along railing tops and connections.

---

## **🛠️ Improvements & Fixes**

- Simplified curved pole geometry while preserving its smooth shape.
- Reused existing textures without adding new texture files.
- Kept gate updates limited to connected, loaded segments.
